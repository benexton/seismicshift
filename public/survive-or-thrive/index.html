How upgrades work

Upgrades

Optional extras that cost money to install but add more than that to the building's value. Your client will put the difference towards seismic upgrades. The catch: they add weight up high, which the earthquake will find. Costs are shown as a share of the building's base price, so they mean the same for a home or an office block.
